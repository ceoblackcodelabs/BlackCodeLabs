About us

A Movement of Hope, Powered by Kenyans.

Our story. Sisi kwa Sisi means “us for us.” It began with a simple conviction: ordinary Kenyans, standing together, can solve problems that no single family should face alone — a hospital bill, a school fee, a home lost to floods.

This proposed platform gives that spirit a home: one trusted place where needs are shared, verified, funded and reported on openly.

Our mission

To mobilise Kenyans to support one another with dignity, speed and complete transparency.

Our vision

A Kenya where no one faces hardship alone, because their community is always within reach.

Why participation matters. A KSh 100 gift and a shared post matter as much as a large donation. Movements are built by many hands, not a few.

How we operate (proposed)

Built on a clear, repeatable process.

The model below is proposed content, to be refined with the Sisi kwa Sisi leadership.

  • Requests submitted online or via community referrers
  • Documents and context reviewed before publishing
  • Funds tracked per campaign, not pooled invisibly
  • Regular updates and a closing report for every campaign
