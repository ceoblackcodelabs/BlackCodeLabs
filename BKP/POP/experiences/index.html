Safari Experiences

Intimate Encounters with the Wild

Game drives, walking safaris, horseback and camel safaris offer different ways to connect deeply with the African wilderness.

Game Drives in Open Safari Vehicles

The heart of the African safari experience

Conducted in open 4x4 vehicles with expert guides, game drives allow you to observe wildlife up close while learning about ecosystems, behavior, and conservation. Flexible, private or semi-private drives with guides who interpret the landscape, not just spot animals.

● Masai Mara● Amboseli● Samburu● Meru National Park
CouplesFamiliesFirst-time Safari GoersWildlife Enthusiasts

Walking Safaris and Guided Bush Walks

A deeper understanding of the African wilderness

Led by experienced guides and rangers, you explore on foot, learning to read tracks, plants, and signs often missed on vehicle safaris. Walking safaris reconnect you with nature at a human pace, creating an intimate and grounding experience.

● Laikipia● Samburu● Meru National Park● Loisaba Conservancy
Adventure SeekersNature LoversSolo TravelersCouples

Horseback Safaris

Move quietly through the landscape among wildlife

Horse safaris allow you to move quietly through the landscape, often approaching wildlife without the presence of vehicles. Ideal for experienced riders and adventurous travelers seeking a unique safari perspective.

● Laikipia● Borana Conservancy● Loisaba Conservancy
Experienced RidersAdventure TravelersCouples

Camel Safaris

A traditional and immersive way to explore

Camel safaris are slow, rhythmic, and deeply grounding. Travel ancient trade routes, spend nights under the stars, and enjoy cultural interaction with local communities in the arid landscapes of northern Kenya.

● Samburu● Northern Kenya Conservancies
Cultural TravelersFamiliesCouplesSlow Travel Enthusiasts
Signature Bush Moments

Experiences That Define Safari Magic

These iconic safari rituals transform a trip into a memory that lasts—sundowners, bush dining, and balloon flights over the savannah.

Sundowners

A classic safari ritual at sunset

Sundowners are enjoyed at scenic viewpoints as the sun sets over the wilderness. These moments invite reflection, connection, and stillness after a day of exploration—where memories are made.

● Masai Mara● Amboseli● Samburu● Laikipia
CouplesHoneymoonersSolo TravelersPhotography Lovers

Bush Breakfasts and Bush Dinners

Dining in the wild elevates a safari experience

Whether it's breakfast under an acacia tree or a candlelit dinner beneath the stars, dining in the wild creates unforgettable moments. Tables set elegantly, fresh gourmet dishes, and the sounds of the African bush.

● Masai Mara● Amboseli● Laikipia● Meru National Park
CouplesHoneymoonersBirthday CelebrationsMultigenerational Families

Balloon Safaris

Floating silently over vast plains at sunrise

A balloon safari is one of the most unforgettable safari experiences. Lift off at sunrise, drift silently over savannahs, watch wildlife from above. Includes champagne bush breakfast and panoramic wildlife viewing.

● Masai Mara
HoneymoonersPhotographersBucket-list AdventurersLuxury Travelers
Adventure and Active Experiences

For Travelers Who Love to Move

Quad biking, cycling safaris, and mountain hikes for those who want to engage actively with Kenya's diverse landscapes.

Quad Biking Safaris

An exhilarating way to explore conservancies

Guided quad biking blends adventure with scenery. Ride across open plains, maneuver through dry riverbeds, traverse rugged terrain, and discover hidden corners unreachable by vehicles.

● Laikipia● Private Conservancies
Adventure SeekersCouplesFamilies with Teens

Cycling and Biking Safaris

Engage actively with landscapes on two wheels

Mountain biking and guided cycling safaris allow you to engage actively with landscapes while maintaining a strong connection to nature. Ride through trails, forests, and savannah paths.

● Hell's Gate National Park● Laikipia
Fit TravelersActive CouplesAdventure Groups

Hiking and Mountain Walks

Showcase Kenya's varied terrain

From gentle nature walks to more challenging hikes, these experiences showcase Kenya's varied terrain—from volcanic craters to forest canopies and dramatic escarpments.

● Mount Kenya● Aberdares● Chyulu Hills● Ngong Hills
Solo TravelersAdventure SeekersFamilies with Older KidsPhotographers
Water and Coastal Experiences

Life Along Rivers, Lakes, and the Ocean

From tranquil boat safaris to deep sea fishing, Kenya's waterways and coastline offer diverse aquatic adventures.

Boat Safaris and Cruises

A tranquil way to observe wildlife

Water-based safaris offer a tranquil way to observe wildlife and landscapes. Glide across calm waters as African fish eagles dive, hippos wallow, and guides narrate the ecosystem's stories.

● Lake Naivasha● Watamu Creek● Lamu Archipelago
FamiliesSeniorsCouplesBird Watchers

River Tubing and Floating Safaris

Relaxing and playful, nature and leisure combined

River tubing is a thrilling yet gentle way to float down Kenya's wild rivers, passing through gorges, pools, and lush riverbanks. Combines fun and adventure with the calmness of drifting through nature.

● Samburu Region● Private Lodges with River Access
FamiliesFriend GroupsYoung AdultsPlayful Couples

Deep Sea Fishing and Sport Fishing

Some of the best fishing in the Indian Ocean

Kenya's coastline offers some of the best fishing in the Indian Ocean. Whether game fishing for marlin or enjoying a peaceful day on the water, the coast delivers.

● Watamu● Malindi● Diani
Fishing EnthusiastsFather-Son TripsAdventure Seekers
