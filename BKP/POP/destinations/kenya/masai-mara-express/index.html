Masai Mara Express Safari

A Classic Big Five Safari in Kenya's Iconic Reserve

Duration3 Days / 2 Nights
Journey typeRoad Safari
RegionMasai Mara
Best timeYear-round (Migration: July–October)
Pace2N Masai Mara

Trip overview

Experience the legendary Masai Mara on this perfectly crafted 3-day safari. From the moment you arrive, you'll be immersed in one of Africa's most celebrated ecosystems — home to the Big Five, vast golden savannahs, and the dramatic Mara River crossings during the Great Migration. This express itinerary is ideal for travellers short on time but big on adventure.

Trip highlights

  • Big Five game drives in the Masai Mara National Reserve
  • Scenic drive through the Great Rift Valley
  • Optional hot air balloon safari at sunrise
  • Bush breakfast in the savannah
  • Sundowner cocktails overlooking the Mara plains
  • Maasai village cultural visit

Choose your comfort level

All prices in KES, per person sharing. Select a level to update your estimate.

Where you’ll stay

Budget

Sentrim Mara Camp

A comfortable tented camp located along the banks of the Talek River, offering authentic safari ambiance with en-suite tents and warm hospitality.

Mid-Range

Mara Sopa Lodge

Set high on the slopes of the Oloolaimutia Hills with panoramic views of the Mara, this lodge blends African architecture with modern comfort.

Luxury

Mara Engai Wilderness Lodge

An intimate luxury lodge perched on the edge of the Mara escarpment, offering breathtaking views of the savannah and exclusive game-viewing experiences.

Day-by-day itinerary

Depart Nairobi early morning and drive through the Great Rift Valley, stopping at the viewpoint for breathtaking panoramic views. Continue through Narok town to the Masai Mara National Reserve, arriving in time for lunch at your camp. After settling in, head out for your first afternoon game drive as the savannah comes alive with wildlife. Return to camp for sundowner drinks and a hearty dinner under the African stars.

Wake before dawn for an optional hot air balloon safari over the Mara (additional cost). After breakfast, embark on a full-day game drive exploring the reserve's diverse landscapes — from open grasslands to riverine forests along the Mara River. Search for lion prides, elephant herds, cheetahs, leopards, and buffalo. Enjoy a bush lunch in the wild before continuing the afternoon drive. Visit a Maasai village in the evening to learn about their traditions and way of life.

Rise early for a final sunrise game drive — the golden light makes for exceptional wildlife photography. After a leisurely bush breakfast, bid farewell to the Mara and begin the scenic drive back to Nairobi, arriving in the late afternoon. Transfer to your hotel or the airport for your onward journey.
