Best time

July – October

Peak season. Great Migration river crossings in the Masai Mara. Dry weather ideal for game viewing.

Best time

January – March

Hot and dry. Excellent wildlife viewing as animals gather around water sources. Beach season at the coast.

Best time

June – September

Cool and dry. Perfect for highland safaris in Laikipia and Samburu. Migration begins in July.

Best time

November – December

Short rains. Fewer crowds, lush green landscapes, and excellent birdwatching. Great value season.
