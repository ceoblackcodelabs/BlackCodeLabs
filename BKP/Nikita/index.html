Who She Is

ABOUT NIKITA

24 Years Old
Nairobi · Born 2002

NIKITA
KERING

Nikita Chepchumba Kering' grew up the fourth of five children, singing before she could properly talk and performing for family by the age of three. Mentored early on by gospel artist Emmy Kosgei, she trained through school at Brookhouse International, where a music scholarship carried her into a diploma in performance, marketing and production.

Her 2018 debut single "Happy With You," released at sixteen, opened the door. A year later, the emPawa Africa masterclass and a deal with Universal Music Africa turned a promising teenager into one of the most closely watched voices in East African R&B — an artist known as much for directing her own visuals as for the vocals themselves.
