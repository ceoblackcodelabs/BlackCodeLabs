Get To Know Her

The Girl Behind the Laughs

Born Mitchelle Joyce Akoth Oruko, Mjaka Mfine grew up between Umoja and Kayole in Nairobi's Eastlands. She started posting funny, relatable skits shortly after finishing high school in 2020 — and it didn't take long for Kenya (and Tanzania) to fall in love with her energy.

Her comedy leans into pure everyday Kenyan chaos: matatu drama, school memories, and the little quirks of city life that everyone secretly relates to. One of her most viral moments came from a matatu skit loudly demanding her KES 50 change — a clip that turned into a nationwide catchphrase moment.

"Fame is nice... and not nice, both at the same time." 💭
